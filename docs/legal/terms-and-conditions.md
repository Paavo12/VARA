## 5. Booking a ride

- **Price.** Before you request a ride, the app shows an exact price for each ride type. That is the price you pay. It does not change for detours, traffic, waiting time or the number of passengers. During busy times, prices may be higher; the higher price is shown before you request.
- **Passengers.** You may book for 1–4 passengers travelling together. All passengers must be women **[and guests must be at least [16] — to be decided]**. FUber never shares your ride with strangers.
- **Booking for someone else.** You may book a ride for a guest. You must have her permission to give us her name and number, she must meet the passenger rules above, and you remain responsible for the booking and payment.
- **PIN.** Your driver will ask for the 4-digit PIN shown in your app (or sent to your guest by SMS). Do not get into a car if the driver, vehicle or plate do not match the app, or if the driver does not ask for your PIN. Never share your PIN with anyone other than your driver at pickup.
- **Scheduled rides.** We try to find a driver for scheduled rides but cannot guarantee one. We will tell you if we cannot.
- **Accessibility.** If you request a wheelchair-accessible vehicle, we offer the ride only to drivers whose vehicles are listed as accessible.

## 6. Cancellations and waiting

- Cancelling is **free** for riders and drivers. We do not charge cancellation or no-show fees.
- Frequent cancellations reduce your internal reliability score, which can mean slower matching. Completing trips restores it.
- When your driver arrives, she may wait **5 minutes** for you free of charge. After that she may cancel, and the cancellation counts as yours.

## 7. Payment

- You can pay by **cash** or **card** (and other methods we add later).
- **Card:** when you request a ride we may place a temporary hold for the price on your card, charge it when the trip ends, and release it if the ride is cancelled. Card details are handled by our payment provider **[name]**; FUber does not store your full card number.
- **Cash:** pay your driver the exact price shown in the app at the end of the trip.
- Receipts are available in the app.
- If you think you were charged incorrectly, report it in the app within **[14]** days. Refunds are at our reasonable discretion, without affecting your rights under consumer law.

We do not guarantee a response time, although we aim to respond to every SOS within 60 seconds.
