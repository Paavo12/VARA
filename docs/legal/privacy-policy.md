### 3.2 Information collected when you use FUber

- **Location.**
  - *Riders:* your device location while you book a ride and during an active ride (only while the app is in use).
  - *Drivers:* your device location while you are online in the Driver app, **including when the app is in the background**, and during trips. We do not collect your location when you are offline.
- **Trip information:** pickup and destination, route, times, distance, fare, ride type, number of passengers, payment status, PIN-entry events, cancellations.
- **Safety information:** SOS alerts, RideCheck events (for example, a long unexpected stop or route deviation), and the records of how our safety team handled them.
- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system, app version, language, crash reports, IP address, and push-notification token.
- **Call information:** when you call through the app's masked line, we record that a call took place, its time and duration. **We do not record call audio.**

### 3.3 Information from others

- From the rider who books you a ride (if you are a guest passenger) or who names you as an emergency contact.
- From our identity-verification provider (the outcome of document and selfie checks), if used.
- From our payment provider (payment status, card brand and last 4 digits).
- From authorities, if they lawfully provide information about a safety incident.

### 3.4 Sensitive information

Identity documents, selfies used to verify your identity, police clearance certificates, and information about your sex/gender (used to determine eligibility for a women-only service) are sensitive. We collect them only to verify identity and eligibility and to keep users safe, we restrict access to a small number of trained staff, and every access is logged. **[Counsel: confirm the lawful basis for processing information about sex/gender and criminal records; consider explicit consent plus legitimate interest/legal obligation.]**

**Automated decisions.** Matching uses an automated score (distance, estimated arrival time, likelihood of accepting, and internal reliability points). You can ask us to explain or review any decision about you.

## 5. Who sees your information

- **Your rider or driver.** Before pickup a driver sees the rider's first name, rating, approximate pickup area and ride details; the exact pickup address when she arrives; and the destination only after the correct PIN is entered. A rider sees the driver's first name, photo, rating, vehicle and plate, and live location while the driver is on the way and during the trip. **Phone numbers are never shared** — calls and messages go through the app.
- **Your emergency contacts and anyone you share a trip with**, when you press SOS or share your trip: your first name, live location, driver's first name, vehicle, plate, destination area and ETA. Share links stop working 30 minutes after the trip ends.
- **Guest passengers** receive the driver's first name, vehicle, plate and the PIN by SMS.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf under written agreements: cloud hosting **[provider, region — e.g. South Africa]**, SMS and push-notification providers, payment provider **[name]**, identity-verification provider **[name, if used]**, masked-calling provider **[name]**, map and routing services, error monitoring, and customer support tools.
- **Authorities**, when required by law, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent harm to someone's life or safety (for example, sharing trip details with the Namibian Police during an SOS).
- **A buyer or successor** if FUber is reorganised, merged or sold, under the same protections.

We **do not sell** your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.

## 8. How we protect your information

Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest; extra encryption for ID numbers, dates of birth, bank details and emergency-contact numbers; strict access controls with two-factor authentication for staff; audit logs of staff access; security testing including independent penetration tests; and data minimisation (for example, hiding exact addresses and destinations until needed). No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Correct** inaccurate information (most of it in the app's Account section).
- **Delete** your account in the app (Account → Delete account) or by contacting us.
- **Object** to or ask us to **restrict** certain processing.
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on it (for example, marketing). This does not affect processing already done.
- **Ask for a human review** of an automated or verification decision.
- **Complain** to us, and to the competent authority **[name, once established]**.

Location permission can be turned off in your phone's settings, but FUber cannot match or track rides without it. Drivers can stop location collection at any time by going offline.

We respond to requests within **[30]** days and may need to verify your identity first. Contact **[privacy@fuber.app]**.
